Easy to miss, hard to forget

On a quiet Kathmandu street, Nepal Momo House lets its glowing sign do the talking. Regulars just follow the smell of the steamer.

Inside, it's simple: a cozy dining room built around one specialty that keeps people coming back.

What's on the table

Momos are the heart of the menu, with a few Himalayan comfort dishes alongside them.

  • Steamed & fried momo

    Soft dumplings with a juicy filling, steamed or fried to order, with a fiery achar on the side.

  • Chicken & veg taipo

    Gigantic steamed buns: the chicken one comes with a hard-boiled egg, the veg one with a cube of cheese.

  • Thukpa

    Tibetan-style noodle soup in a warm chicken or veg broth, made for a cold evening.

  • Aloo-dum bhuja

    Darjeeling-style spicy potatoes tempered with black cumin and served hot with bhuja.

What regulars say

Regulars rave about the juicy filling, and an achar rich enough to eat on its own.

It fills up fast on weekends, so come early — nobody minds the wait once the plate arrives.
