Easy to miss, hard to forget

Up a short flight of stairs off a quiet Kathmandu lane, Fold & Steam doesn't shout for attention. Regulars just know the glowing sign and the smell of the steamer.

Inside, it's simple: a cozy dining room built around one specialty that keeps people climbing those stairs.

What's on the table

The menu covers the usual Nepali-Tibetan favorites, but people come here for one dish in particular.

  • The house momo

    Hand-folded and steamed to order, served with a dark, well-spiced achar for dipping. It's the dish the rest of the menu is built around.

  • Buff specialties

    Buffalo meat dishes are a mainstay here, cooked home-style at prices that keep locals coming back.

  • Chowmein & noodles

    A reliable plate to share alongside the momo — the two are usually ordered together.

  • Milk tea

    Strong, sweet, and served the way it should be — best with a plate of something fried.

What regulars say

Regulars rave about the juicy filling, and an achar rich enough to eat on its own.

It fills up fast on weekends, so come early — nobody minds the wait once the plate arrives.
